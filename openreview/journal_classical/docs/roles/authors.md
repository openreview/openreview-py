# Authors

## Your Work

- Submit new JMLR manuscripts and resubmissions.
- Keep the Author List, affiliations, and OpenReview profile identities accurate.
- Respond to required author tasks, especially camera-ready revision requests after acceptance.
- Read decisions, released reviews, and official paper communication when they become available.

## Where To Go

Use the venue landing page's `New Submission` entry point to submit a new manuscript. You do not need pre-existing `JMLR/Authors` membership to submit; the submitted Author List determines who becomes an author for the paper.

Use the previous paper page's `Start Resubmission` author action after a Regular-paper rejection that explicitly permits resubmission. That action opens the standard submission editor, clearly labeled as a linked resubmission. It creates a new paper and forum, while showing the inherited Regular track and previous paper as context. Add every author through OpenReview's searchable Authors profile selector. The new paper page shows one `Previous JMLR submission` link to its immediate predecessor; it does not reproduce the whole resubmission chain.

Use the Author Console to find papers where you are listed as an author and to complete author tasks. Paper links open the standard OpenReview paper page.

## Main Actions

| Action | When to use it |
|---|---|
| New Submission | Submit a new manuscript from the venue submission entry point. |
| Resubmission | Submit a revised manuscript from the previous paper page after an eligible decision. |
| Author List | Enter every author by OpenReview profile ID, in paper order, for example `~First_Author1, ~Second_Author1`. Every author must have a profile ID, and the submitting author's profile ID must be included. |
| Cover letter context | Describe unusual editorial circumstances when useful. The cover letter is contextual only and does not create assignment-conflict records; OpenReview's conflict result remains authoritative. |
| Official Comment | Send paper-specific communication to selected permitted readers. To write to the handling editor, select the paper Authors and Action Editors and do not select Reviewers. |
| Camera-ready revision | Upload final material after acceptance when requested. |

## Notes

Submission and resubmission field rules are owned by the
[lifecycle](../workflow/lifecycle.md) and visible submission form.

JMLR review is single blind. Editors and reviewers can see author identities, but authors should not see reviewer or Action Editor identities.

For a resubmission, use the previous paper page action. `Accept with minor revision` instead uses Camera Ready Revision on the same paper. The Action Editor's decision comments identify the requested changes. Upload the revised final PDF and post a restricted Official Comment summarizing how you addressed those changes. The Action Editor verifies the revision without another reviewer round. The decision email links to the paper forum and gives the configured camera-ready deadline.

If a button or task is missing, first check that you are in the Author Console role context and on the correct paper page.
